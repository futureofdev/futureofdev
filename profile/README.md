## What is Claude Academy?

Claude Academy is a completely free, open-source coding bootcamp built by [Future of Dev](https://futureofdev.com). Every course uses Claude as your teacher — not a shortcut to copy-paste answers, but a genuine guide that builds real understanding.

No enrolment windows. No waitlists. No cost. Start today.

## What we cover

- **Software development** — From zero to shipping real projects. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, APIs — actual understanding, not copy-paste.
- **Getting more from Claude** — Learn to prompt, direct, and collaborate with Claude so you can multiply what you get done.
- **Agentic workflows** — Understand how AI agents work and design workflows where they handle the repetitive work.
- **Product management** — Define, prioritise, and ship better products using AI to research, write specs, and move faster.
- **Data & analytics** — Explore, clean, and interpret data with Claude as your co-analyst — no data science degree needed.
- **Design & content** — Sharpen your ideas, write better copy, and communicate more clearly with AI in the loop.

## How it works

**Claude is your teacher, not a shortcut.**
Every course is designed so you understand what you're building and why — not just accepting whatever Claude produces.

**Always open, always free.**
Every lesson is open-source — fork it, remix it, share it. No paywall, ever.

**Built for where the market is heading.**
These courses teach you to work alongside AI the way real teams already do — with intention and control.
