# Write in the Future of Dev voice

Create public-facing copy that sounds like the shipped work and serves the
approved audience strategy.

## Establish the brief

Read `voice.md` and the relevant part of `docs/strategy/relaunch-strategy.md`.
Identify:

- surface: newsletter, insight, site/product copy or founder-led social;
- primary audience: Evolve by default, Enter or Lead only when the brief says;
- one change, skill or decision the piece must make useful;
- real evidence available: an artefact, observation, tool, protocol, result or
  source;
- the single next action.

Ask for missing evidence when inventing it would change the claim. Never invent
experience, reader proof, testimonials, outcomes, dates or product support.

## Draft

Follow the register for the selected surface in `voice.md`. Lead with a concrete
situation or clear point. Translate technical terms on first use without
talking down. Resolve the idea into something the reader can do, check or build.

For a newsletter edition, use the standard block sequence when it fits: The
Shift, Why It Matters, Learn, Build, Keep and Go Deeper. Go Deeper has one call
to action.

For the required LinkedIn post, make it useful on its own and point to the
canonical insight URL with:

- `utm_source=linkedin`
- `utm_medium=social`
- an issue-specific `utm_campaign`

Do not promise a relaunch date, weekday or catalogue delivery date unless the
canonical strategy has been explicitly updated.

## Review

Apply the stop-phrase and punctuation rules in `voice.md`, then score the draft
for Directness, Rhythm, Trust, Authenticity and Density. Revise any draft below
35/50 before presenting it.

Check the final copy for:

- a real named thing or observed moment;
- one earned takeaway;
- no fear-based career marketing;
- no unsupported compatibility claim;
- no hand-authored course counts;
- no placeholder proof;
- no private metric or subscriber information.

Return the draft and the compact score. Do not publish it or mutate an external
platform without separate authorization.
