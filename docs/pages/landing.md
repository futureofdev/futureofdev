# Future of Dev - Homepage PRD

## 1. Purpose
The homepage introduces **Future of Dev**, a learning & support focused on the evolving world of software development in an AI-driven era. It must:

- Immediately communicate the **mission**: helping students, graduates, and employees across all roles understand AI’s impact and build future-proof skills.  
- Encourage visitors to **register for insights, courses, webinars, and bootcamps**.  
- Showcase thought leadership and free content while positioning premium offerings.  

---

## 2. Audience

- **Students / Graduates** entering tech  
- **Junior / Mid-level Developers** (this is the ICP)
- **All roles in software development lifecycle** (PMs, QA, UX/UI, DevOps, BAs)  
- **Tech professionals curious about AI's impact in their roles**  

---

## 3. Key Sections / Content Blocks

### **A. Hero Section**
- **Purpose:** Immediate clarity on what Future of Dev is and why it matters.  
- **Elements:**  
  - Headline: *“Goodbye, World: Navigate the AI-Driven Future of Software Careers”*  
  - Subheadline: *“Understand AI’s impact, gain the right skills, and future-proof your career.”*  
  - CTA Button: *“Join the Free Webinar”*  
  - Optional hero visual / illustration: abstract “software lifecycle + AI” graphic  

---

### **B. Mission / About Section**
- **Purpose:** Explain why Future of Dev exists and your personal credibility.  
- **Elements:**  
  - Short paragraph on the founder’s experience and insights  
  - Two core passions:  
    1. Helping people grow in their careers and teams  
    2. Helping newcomers enter tech with the right skills and guidance  
  - CTA / link to *“Learn More / Our Story”*  

---

### **C. Featured Webinar / Lead Capture**
- **Purpose:** Drive registration for first webinar and capture leads.  
- **Elements:**  
  - Title: *“Goodbye, World: How AI is Changing Software Careers Forever”*  
  - Description of topics: AI’s impact on roles, key skills, actionable steps  
  - CTA: *“Register for Free”*
  - Secondary: Read Insight
  - Optional countdown timer or date/time  

---

### **D. Free Content / Insights**
- **Purpose:** Show thought leadership, encourage repeat visits, support SEO.  
- **Elements:**  
  - Blog posts / insights list (title + short excerpt + link)  
  - Optional filters by audience / role  
  - CTA to subscribe to newsletter  

---

### **F. Social Proof / Testimonials**
- **Purpose:** Build credibility and trust  
- **Elements:**  
  - Quotes from past attendees, students, or industry leaders  
  - Logos / mentions if available  

---

### **G. Footer**
- **Purpose:** Utility, engagement, secondary links  
- **Elements:**  
  - Newsletter signup form  
  - Social links  
  - Legal / privacy links  

---

## 4. Features
- Design should be modern, clean and white with black text
- Typography choices should be reflective of audience, with modern code-inspired choices
- Simple and minimalist, no logo or top navigation bar
- Responsive design for mobile / desktop  
- Newsletter capture and integration with email platform  
- Webinar registration capture  
- Highlight free content and premium offerings  
- SEO-friendly blog layout  
- Optional interactive elements: polls, skill quizzes, role selector  

---

## 5. Success Metrics
- **Primary:** Webinar sign-ups  
- **Secondary:** Blog post engagement, repeat visitors  
- **Tertiary:** Social shares, referral traffic  

---

## 6. Notes
- Keep copy **inclusive for all roles** in the software lifecycle  
- Emphasize **AI impact + future-proof skills** throughout  
- Use **hero CTA** for highest-priority conversion (webinar / newsletter)
